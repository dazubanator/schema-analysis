Figure: Sensor Coordination (Pearson Correlation) Across Experimental Phases.

This bar chart compares mean Pearson Correlation Coefficient (r) values between the Manipulated Arm (Sensor 1) and the Subject Matching Arm (Sensor 2) for the Follow Me and Vibration phases in the high-vividness cohort (N=7).

For both phases, each participant's two arm-angle signals were first time-aligned using bounded cross-correlation (maximum lag 2.5 s), and correlation was then computed in the matched 10-25 s phase window.

The y-axis shows Pearson r (-1 to 1), with error bars indicating SEM. A paired t-test across participants showed a significant phase difference (t=3.912, p=0.0079).

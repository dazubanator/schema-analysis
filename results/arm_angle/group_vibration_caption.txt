Figure: Group Average Angular Displacement During the Vibration Phase (Illusory Drift).

This figure contrasts the independent physical displacement of both arms during the kinesthetic illusion stimulation phase (N=7 participants).

The x-axis represents time in seconds aligned to vibration onset (T=0). The y-axis displays absolute physical displacement from each participant's baseline orientation at T=0 in degrees.

The blue line (#1996FC) represents the Manipulated Arm (Sensor 1), which remains near zero displacement throughout the trial.
The orange line (#FD8164) represents the Subject Matching Arm (Sensor 2), which shows progressive drift as participants track the illusory movement of the manipulated arm.

Solid lines denote the respective group means, and the shaded regions represent the boundaries of the standard error of the mean (SEM).

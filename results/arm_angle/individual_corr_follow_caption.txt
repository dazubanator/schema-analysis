Figure: Subject-Level Variance in Sensor Coordination During the Follow Me Phase.

This figure shows the Pearson Correlation Coefficient (r) for each of the 7 valid participants during the Follow Me tracking phase.

The x-axis identifies individual participants and the y-axis shows each participant's Pearson r value from the 10-25 s phase window after bounded lag alignment (maximum lag 2.5 s). The red dashed line denotes the group mean.

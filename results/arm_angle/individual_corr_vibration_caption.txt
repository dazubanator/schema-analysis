Figure: Subject-Level Variance in Sensor Coordination During the Vibration Phase.

This figure shows individual variance in Pearson Correlation Coefficient (r) for the 7 participants during the Vibration phase (illusory drift).

Consistent with the main comparison analysis, signals were time-aligned by bounded cross-correlation (maximum lag 2.5 s), and correlation was calculated in the 10-25 s window. The x-axis identifies each participant and the y-axis shows their Pearson r value.

The broad intra-subject variance and lower correlation magnitudes relative to Follow Me indicate heterogeneous susceptibility to the kinesthetic illusion. The red dashed line denotes the group mean.
